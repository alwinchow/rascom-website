About Rascom

We started by building a queue. We never stopped engineering for the real world.

Rascom Technology Sdn. Bhd. (1260877-H) was incorporated in 2018 in Petaling Jaya, alongside two partner companies that still shape how we build software today.

Origin

From queue tickets to a CRM platform

2018

Three companies, one venture

Rascom Technology is incorporated alongside Elid Sdn. Bhd. and Expedio Design Sdn. Bhd. — a software house paired with a hardware manufacturer and a product design studio.

2018–2019

A mobile queue-management system

Our first project: a mobile app for queue management, fully funded by Elid's Dr. Wong. Elid handled the hardware design and manufacturing side; Expedio shaped the look, feel and branding. The project was later cancelled before launch.

2019–2025

Software customization & maintenance

Rascom shifted to building and maintaining web and mobile software for Elid's hardware products, and delivering software for design installations originated by Expedio — including Kinexus, Sensing Cart and Footbraille.

2026 →

Cloud CRM

We're now building a Cloud CRM for freelancers, insurance agents, real estate agents and electrical installers — set to launch late 2026 or early 2027.
